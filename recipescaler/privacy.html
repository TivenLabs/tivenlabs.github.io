Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Overview

RecipeScaler ("the App") is developed by Tiven Labs. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App operates entirely on your device. Specifically:

  • No account or login required
  • No personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are included
  • No cookies or advertising identifiers are used

Camera & Photo Library

The App may request access to your camera or photo library to scan recipe cards. Photos are processed entirely on your device using Apple's built-in Vision framework. No images are uploaded, transmitted, or stored beyond your device.

Data Storage

Saved recipes are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data is not accessible to us or any third party. If you delete the App, all saved data is permanently removed.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs these transactions.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since we collect no personal data from any user, this policy applies equally to all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@tivenlabs.com.
