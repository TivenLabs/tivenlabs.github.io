The honest answers

Is it free?

Yep. Scaling recipes, scanning cards, and the unit converter are all free to use. There's an optional one-time $1.99 Pro upgrade (not a subscription) if you want unlimited saved recipes, a shopping list, and a full-screen Cook Mode that keeps your screen awake while you cook.

Does it work without internet?

It does. Everything runs right on your iPhone — no account, no sign-in, no connection needed. You can scale a recipe on a plane or in a cabin with zero bars.

How does scanning a recipe actually work?

You take a photo of a printed recipe — a card, a cookbook page, a printout. The app reads the ingredients using Apple's on-device text recognition and drops them into a list you can scale instantly. The photo never leaves your phone.

Can it handle grams and cups?

Yes — the built-in converter moves between cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, fluid ounces, milliliters, liters, grams, kilograms, ounces, and pounds. And it shows everything as tidy fractions instead of long decimals.

What about my privacy?

There's nothing to worry about here. No data collection, no tracking, no ads, no account. Your recipes and photos stay on your device, full stop.
